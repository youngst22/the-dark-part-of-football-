Not Another Football Gossip Page.

THE DARK PART OF FOOTBALL is built around researched football storytelling. The goal is to follow the timeline, find the original reporting, compare accounts and explain what is actually known.

A viral post is not automatically a fact. A rumour is not automatically a story. And a controversial headline deserves context.

The strongest case files give readers the information needed to understand what happened — not simply repeat what everyone else is saying.

Our Editorial Promise

We distinguish confirmed facts from allegations, reports and analysis.

Claims about real people are attributed to credible sources and are not knowingly presented as facts when they cannot be established.

We do not knowingly invent football stories, quotes, scandals or allegations.

How a Case File Is Built

The research system behind THE DARK PART OF FOOTBALL.

01 — Find Collect reputable interviews, club statements, established news reports, biographies and other reliable sources.
02 — Verify Separate confirmed information from claims, rumours and speculation.
03 — Timeline Put important events in chronological order so the reader understands what happened and when.
04 — Explain Add context and original analysis instead of simply rewriting another website.
05 — Attribute Important claims should be connected to their sources so readers can understand where the information came from.
06 — Update When significant new information changes a story, the article should be updated rather than left misleading.
