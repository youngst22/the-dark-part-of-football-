Not another football gossip page.

THE DARK PART OF FOOTBALL is designed for long-form football storytelling: follow the timeline, find the original reporting, compare conflicting accounts and explain what is actually known.

Our strongest stories won't simply repeat a viral post. They'll investigate why the story exists in the first place.

Editorial note

Allegations are labelled as allegations and attributed to their source. Rumours are not presented as established facts. We do not knowingly invent claims about real people. Readers should be able to distinguish reporting, allegations, confirmed facts and analysis.

How a case file is built

This is the system to use when turning a footballer into a deep-dive story.

01 — FindCollect reputable interviews, club statements, public records where appropriate, biographies and established news reports.
02 — VerifySeparate what is confirmed from what is merely reported or alleged. Never upgrade a rumour into a fact.
03 — TimelinePut important events in chronological order so readers understand what happened and when.
04 — ExplainGive readers original analysis and context instead of simply rewriting another website.
05 — AttributeClearly identify where important claims came from and avoid anonymous internet gossip presented as evidence.
06 — UpdateIf a significant new fact changes a story, update the article instead of silently leaving an outdated claim.
