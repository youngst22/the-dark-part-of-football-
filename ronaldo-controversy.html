Cristiano Ronaldo: The Controversy That Followed Football's Biggest Star

A documented timeline of the 2009 allegation, the 2010 settlement, the criminal investigation, the civil lawsuit and the later court rulings.

THE DARK PART OF FOOTBALL • Research article
Important: This article distinguishes allegations from established legal findings. Cristiano Ronaldo denied the allegation and maintained that the sexual encounter was consensual. He was not criminally charged over the allegation.

Cristiano Ronaldo's career has been built on extraordinary footballing success: goals, trophies, records and global fame. But one controversy followed the Portuguese star for more than a decade and eventually became a major legal battle in the United States.

The case concerns an alleged sexual assault in Las Vegas in 2009. Because the matter involved both a criminal investigation and later civil litigation, its history is more complicated than a simple headline can suggest.

2009: The Las Vegas allegation

Ronaldo and Kathryn Mayorga met in Las Vegas in 2009. Mayorga later alleged that Ronaldo sexually assaulted her in a hotel room. Ronaldo denied the allegation and said the encounter was consensual.

The dispute did not immediately become the worldwide story it would later become. A confidential settlement was reached in 2010. Court records later confirmed that Ronaldo paid Mayorga $375,000 under the settlement and that the parties agreed to confidentiality and non-disparagement terms.

2017–2018: The story returns

The controversy resurfaced years later after documents connected to the Football Leaks disclosures became public. The documents included material relating to the earlier claim.

In 2018, Mayorga publicly revived the allegations and filed a civil lawsuit. Las Vegas police also reopened their investigation. Ronaldo continued to deny wrongdoing.

2019: No criminal charges

In July 2019, the Clark County District Attorney's Office announced that Ronaldo would not face criminal charges. Prosecutors said the allegation could not be proven beyond a reasonable doubt based on the information available to them.

That decision is important because it means the allegation did not result in a criminal prosecution or conviction against Ronaldo.

2022: The civil case is dismissed

The separate civil case continued after the criminal investigation ended. In June 2022, a federal judge in Nevada dismissed Mayorga's lawsuit with prejudice.

The court's decision focused heavily on the way Mayorga's lawyer had obtained and used documents that had been stolen through a cyberattack and that included material protected by attorney-client privilege. The judge concluded that case-terminating sanctions were appropriate.

2023: The appeal fails

Mayorga appealed the dismissal. On November 21, 2023, the U.S. Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit affirmed the district court's decision.

The appellate court concluded that the district court had not abused its discretion in dismissing the case as a sanction for the conduct surrounding the use of the privileged documents. The ruling did not find Ronaldo guilty of the original allegation; rather, it upheld the dismissal of the civil lawsuit on procedural and litigation-conduct grounds.

What can actually be established?

This is where the story needs careful wording.

The original sexual-assault allegation was serious, but an allegation is not the same thing as a criminal conviction. Ronaldo denied the allegation. Prosecutors did not bring criminal charges, saying the available evidence could not establish the case beyond a reasonable doubt.

The later civil lawsuit was dismissed by a federal judge, and the Ninth Circuit affirmed that dismissal in 2023. The appellate ruling centered on the improper use of privileged documents and the conduct of the litigation.

The bottom line: Ronaldo was never criminally convicted over the allegation. The civil lawsuit was ultimately dismissed and that dismissal was affirmed on appeal. The controversy nevertheless became one of the most significant off-field legal stories of his career.
